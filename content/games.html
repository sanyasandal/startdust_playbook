Traditional outsource review compresses a 3D model into a flat turntable render - stripping the material detail and geometry your team actually needs to give useful feedback. Playbook streams the live asset directly to every reviewer, on any device, at once.

Playbook + Miris streams the real asset — characters, environments, props — to any device. A creative director on a laptop and an external partner on a tablet see the same full-fidelity model.
