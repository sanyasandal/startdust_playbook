TL;DR: The Playbook Figma plugin lets you browse your whole Playbook library from inside Figma and drag assets straight onto the canvas. No downloading, no re-uploading, no hunting through a Drive folder in another tab. Install the plugin, sign in once, open it from any Figma file, and drop in the logo, photo, or export you need. As Playbook's own plugin copy puts it, "Say goodbye to the hassle of downloading and re-uploading files."

How to pull files from Playbook into Figma

The Playbook Figma plugin connects your design file to your asset library in four steps:

  1. Install the plugin. Add it from the Apps and plugins menu in your Playbook workspace, or from Figma's plugin directory.
  2. Sign in. Open the plugin and sign in to your Playbook workspace once.
  3. Open it from any Figma file. Launch the plugin where you are designing. Your boards and assets show up right in the panel.
  4. Drag and drop. Pull any asset from the plugin onto the canvas. Assets brought in as Figma links also get an "Open in Figma" action and an in-app Figma preview back in Playbook, so the two stay connected.

That is the whole loop. The approved, current version lives in Playbook, and it is one drag away inside Figma. When the asset changes in your library, you are always placing the latest one instead of a stale copy that someone downloaded last month.

What else connects to Playbook

Figma is one of many connections. Playbook also brings in files from Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive, auto-saves your Midjourney generations, posts board activity to Slack and Discord, fires automations through Zapier, clips files from any web page with the Playbook Image Saver Chrome extension, and two-way syncs with Adobe apps through the desktop app. For the full tour, see all Playbook integrations.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to download files from Playbook to use them in Figma?

No. The plugin drops assets straight onto the Figma canvas from your library, so there is no download-then-reupload step. The version in Playbook is always the one you place.

Where do I get the Playbook Figma plugin?

Install it from the Apps and plugins menu in your Playbook workspace or from Figma's plugin directory, then sign in to your workspace once.
