How does migrating from Air to Playbook work?

Migration runs through a dedicated Air connection, so you are not exporting and re-uploading files by hand. The move comes down to three things:

  1. Connect Air. Generate an API key in your Air workspace and connect it to Playbook.
  2. Playbook brings your library across. Your assets come over with their boards and folder structure, and tags travel with them on eligible plans, so the organization you already built carries into Playbook.
  3. Desktop sync. Users who want speed but control on their migration can also migrate via the Playbook Desktop app, which syncs your uploads directly with your local assets.
  4. Review and go live. The transfer runs in the background and is tracked, so you can watch it progress, then invite your team and start working.

The result is a library in Playbook that looks like the one you left, not a flat pile of files you have to sort again.

How long does the migration take?

Most migrations take very little time. If you have access to Air API, a typical library comes across quickly, often within about a week, though the exact timing depends on how much you are moving. At the time of writing, Playbook offers white-glove migration for teams on annual Business plans, where the team handles the move end to end. If you are on another plan or simply want a second set of eyes, reach out with your migration questions and Playbook will walk through your library and the best way to bring it over.

What comes across from Air?

Your assets, your structure, and your metadata come across together. That means the files themselves, the boards and folders you organized them into, and the tags attached to them on eligible plans. Once your library lands, Playbook's AI auto-tagging on paid plans can enrich it further, describing your images and video by their actual content so search works from day one. You keep what you built and gain a layer on top of it.

What happens after you land in Playbook?

Once your library is in, Playbook is a full workspace around it, not just storage. You can search your assets by describing them, share branded galleries and boards with clients, and invite your whole team. Guests stay free on paid plans, so reviewers and clients can comment and download without taking a seat. Consumer brands, agencies, and studios run their entire creative libraries this way.
