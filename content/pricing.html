    Frequently asked questions

    What is Playbook?

    Think of Playbook as your creative HQ: a visual-first space to store, organize, preview, and share files beautifully (images, video, docs and more). Playbook is designed to support all your creative workflows whether you're solo freelancer, agency or part of a global team.

    How do Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans differ?

    Pro is for solo freelancers or small projects that need powerful storage, advanced sharing features

    Team is for small or growing teams that requires more flexible permissions, collaboration and limited AI-powered features (search, tagging, and more!)

    Enterprise is for large and global teams that need org-ready controls like unlimited members, view-only roles, SSO, white-glove onboarding, and API/SDK access.

    Not sure which plan is right for you? Schedule a demo with our team, we'd love to help!

    Do plans apply per workspace or per account?

    Plans apply per workspace you're the Owner of. If you own multiple workspaces, you can update them independently. Allowing you to manage projects and teams separately.

    Is there a Playbook mobile app or desktop app?

    Yes! Playbook is available on web, and also has mobile apps for both iOS and Android. On the desktop side, we also offer a Desktop Sync app (two-way syncing is available to Paid plans only).

    Can I switch or cancel my plans anytime?

    Yes! Just head to your workspace's Settings page and click on Billing to view your current plan and manage your subscription. Changes take effect at the end of your billing cycle.

    What happens if I reach my storage limit?

    Free: The Free plan includes 300 total assets or 100GB of storage, whichever comes first. Once you hit either limit, you'll need to upgrade to Pro, Team, or Enterprise to keep adding new files.

    Pro, Team or Enterprise: You can keep working with no interruption. If your workspace exceeds its included storage, we automatically add additional storage in 1TB increments for $25/month.
    Any existing files will remain accessible and downloadable anytime even after your workspace is over the limit.

    What types of files does Playbook support?

    You can upload nearly any file type to Playbook, we supports virtually all common formats for images (JPEG, PNG, GIF, SVG, TIFF, etc.), videos (MP4 MOV, AVI, etc.), audio (MP3, WAV, etc.), documents (PDF, PSD, AI, etc.), and more.

    Are there any upload/download limits?

    Free: Up to 300 files can be uploaded. Video uploads are capped at 2GB per file, with a 10GB limit per download session.
    Pro: No upload limits, with up to 100GB per download session
    Team/Enterprise: No upload limits, with up to 1000GB per download session

    Keep in mind that these limits will not get in the way of your normal workflow, just to protect us and our community from abuse.

    What are mini-apps?

    Mini-apps are built-in tools, made available right inside your workspace via our partnership with Photoroom, Picsart, etc. With mini-apps, you can access everything you need to complete your projects without ever leaving Playbook. Included in Pro, Team, and Enterprise plans.

    How secure is Playbook overall?

    Playbook prioritizes security with encryption at rest, two-factor authentication, secure backups, and malware scanning. We use industry-standard encryption so that your files are private, unless explicitly shared with others.

    Where can I get help if I have more questions?

    Check out our helpful guides or reach us anytime at support@playbook.com. We're here to help! You can also join our Discord to ask questions and get tips from our community.
