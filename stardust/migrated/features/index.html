Playbook's core features

Individual creatives or teams of any size can manage their projects, programs, and processes, no matter how small or large.

Start for freeBook a demo

See everything. Instantly.

Playbook is cloud storage for creatives that is designed to bring visual clarity to your library. Whether you're browsing on desktop or mobile, the experience is the same: fast, beautiful, and built for your eyes. It's storage that works at the speed of your inspiration.

AI-powered conversational search

We often remember what a file looks like, and not necessarily what you named it three months ago. Our visual inteligence automatically scans every asset to assign AI tags that perfectly capture not only the main subject but also the overall scene. Just type in what you remember–like "blue sky with clouds"–and Playbook handles the guess work for you.

Lighting speed collaboration

Stay on track with your projects by bringing your team, vendors, external stakeholders into a unified workspace. Or specific board with Guests so they always have the right context without accessing your entire workspace. It's a secure, organized way to mange handoffs or internal reviews.

Approve faster. Launch cleaner.

Keep the feedback loop inside your library instead of getting lost in endless email chain. Stakeholders can leave timecodes comments on videos or specific feedbacks on images so the context stays attached to the assets. Live-syncing ensures you can see the lastest comment the moment it's added.

Client-ready sharing in seconds

Create shareable links for boards or individual asset so anyone can review and download assets quickly. While our Publish feature transforms your board into standalone web-pages. Choose from 30+ templates to present your work in a way that's actually impressive and beautiful.

Save money as your library grows

Playbook identifies exact duplicates on upload and removes any extras so you always have a single source of truth. While your related files (i.e. same logo with several different formats) will automatically be stacked under a single group to keep your boards always browseable.
