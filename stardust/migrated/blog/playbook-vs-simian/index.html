What is Simian?

Simian is a cloud-based media sharing and review platform built for companies that create, shoot, edit, and finish advertising content: production studios, ad agencies, and creative firms. It centers on AI-tagged showreels, time-stamped commenting, and real-time viewer analytics.

Strengths:

  • AI-powered tagging that locates specific shots and scenes inside showreels quickly
  • Real-time viewer analytics showing which scenes a producer watched, rewatched, or skipped
  • Time-stamped commenting and version comparison for structured review
  • Adobe Creative Cloud plugin and mobile apps (Simian Go!, Simian Projects) for reel builds on the go

Limitations:

  • Company plans start at $99/month for 10 seats; growing past that or unlocking SSO and developer API access requires an Enterprise quote
  • Built around the showreel and the review cycle around it, with less built for storing and searching a full production archive over time
  • No published template gallery for turning a board into a full branded destination beyond a microsite

Rating: 4.3 out of 5 based on a small sample of reviews on G2 at the time of writing; treat this as directional given the low review count.

Pricing: Simian Lite is $49/month for one seat, Company starts at $99/month for 10 seats, and Enterprise (unlimited users, SSO, developer API access) is quote-only, per Simian's pricing page.

What Playbook does that Simian doesn't

Playbook isn't a showreel tool with storage attached. It's a visual home for creative work where review, search, sharing, and building your own workflow are all one motion inside the same library, not a separate reel-building step.

Timestamp and position commenting, down to the pixel

Comments in Playbook auto-attach to the exact frame and timecode on video, or pin to a precise point, box, or freeform region on an image, so notes point at the exact spot and moment that needs a change. Dorst & Lesser, a 75-person social agency in Amsterdam producing roughly 400 assets a week across 40 clients, called this out directly: "One feature which is very nice is how you can give feedback per second," said co-founder Robert Withagen, per the Dorst & Lesser story. The agency also gets original-quality downloads for posting, a gap it hit with its previous DAM.

Video frame search across the whole library, not just the reel

Simian's AI tagging helps you find a shot inside a showreel you built. Playbook's video frame search works across your entire library: every uploaded video is automatically split into AI-tagged, vectorized shots, so a natural-language search surfaces the right moment from any clip, not just the ones already curated into a reel (Team plan and up). That means archived dailies and raw cuts stay just as findable as the polished reel.

Frame scrubbing and side-by-side asset comparison

Playbook's player offers instant, frame-accurate scrubbing with a thumbnail preview on hover, so a producer can land on the exact frame under discussion without a full download. Version history keeps every cut layered on the same asset record instead of a folder of spot_v6_FINAL files, with a side-by-side compare view so two edits, or two regional versions of the same spot, can play against each other before sign-off. WBD Sports, managing broadcast marketing across TNT Sports, Eurosport, and Bleacher Report, moved legal review onto timestamped video comments and cut review rounds from four to two, with asset requests down about 50 percent, per the WBD Sports story.

Shared pages and a template gallery, not just a microsite

A Simian microsite covers a pitch deck. A Playbook published page is a full branded webpage: choose from 1000+ free templates, customize typography, cover image, and logo through a drag-and-drop page builder, and publish a client gallery, portfolio, or brand portal in one click. A slideshow/presentation mode plays a board full-screen for a pitch, and the internal board and public page share the same content, so a late-breaking edit shows up for the client without resending a link.

Build your own workflow with the Playbook API and MCP

Simian's developer API sits behind the Enterprise tier. Playbook's REST API and MCP support are available starting on the Pro plan: read and mutate assets, boards, comments, and search programmatically, or connect an MCP-capable agent (Claude, Cursor, and others) so it can organize incoming footage, kick off a review request, or sync approved cuts into a delivery system from a prompt, no waiting on a vendor's roadmap.

Pricing and access

Playbook publishes every tier: Free (100GB, 300 assets, no card), Pro at $120/year, Team at $240/year/member (video frame search, video frame scrubbing, and AI tagging start here), Business at $4,800/year flat with unlimited members and version history, and custom Enterprise. Guests comment, review, and download on every plan without taking a paid seat, unlike a per-seat review tool.

What to look for in a Simian alternative

  1. Does search cover your whole archive, not just the reel you built? If a search only finds shots you already curated, the raw footage behind it stays effectively lost.
  2. Is pricing published past the entry tier? A quote wall at the point you need SSO or an API is a budgeting problem waiting to happen.
  3. Is feedback pinned to a frame or a pixel? Precise, timecoded, positioned comments remove the round-trips vague notes create.
  4. What happens to the footage after the reel ships? A tool built only around the showreel needs somewhere for everything else to live.
  5. Can you build on it before Enterprise? An API and MCP layer available from an early tier means you can automate a workflow without a six-figure contract first.
