What is Wiredrive?

Wiredrive is a cloud-based media sharing platform built for agencies, production companies, and brand teams to store, review, and present video and creative work. It centers on secure online review and approval: time-stamped comments, permission-gated screening rooms, and custom-branded reels and quicklinks for pitches.

Strengths:

  • Bulletproof 4K, 8K, and 360-degree playback built specifically for heavy video files
  • Time-stamped comments and approval tracking for structured review chains
  • Custom branded reels, slideshows, and quicklinks for client-facing presentations
  • SSO and link expiration for agencies handling unreleased campaign footage

Limitations:

  • Pricing is not published; plans start around $350 a month and scale by quote, so you cannot budget without a sales call
  • No public API or MCP layer for connecting the library to your own tools or agents
  • Built around the review moment. Once a project wraps, there's no clear home for the footage or a way to search inside it later

Rating: 4.3 out of 5 based on 253 reviews on G2 at the time of writing.

Pricing: Reported starting price is around $350/month, with full plan details available only by contacting Wiredrive directly.

What Playbook does that Wiredrive doesn't

Playbook isn't a review tool with storage attached. It's a visual home for creative work where review, video search, sharing, and building your own workflow are all one motion inside the same library.

Timestamp and position commenting, down to the pixel

A comment in Playbook attaches to the exact frame and timecode automatically, or pins to a precise point, box, or freeform region on an image so feedback points at the pixel, not the file. "Make the logo bigger" is ambiguous. A box drawn around the logo at 00:42 is not. Invictus Media, a seven-person production house in Des Moines that moved off Dropbox and now manages 33,000+ assets in Playbook, invites clients straight into the board to review and pull files themselves: it has "streamlined many of our hand-off processes and relationships," per the Invictus Media story.

Search inside the video, not just for the file

Wiredrive finds the clip. Playbook finds the moment inside it. Every uploaded video is automatically split into AI-tagged, vectorized shots, so a search for "golden hour drone shot" surfaces the exact segment from within a longer master, shown alongside image results in the same AI Search list (Team plan and up). Instead of scrubbing timelines from memory, you search the moment directly.

Frame scrubbing and side-by-side asset comparison

Playbook's player streams adaptive HLS with instant frame-accurate scrubbing and a thumbnail preview on hover, so a reviewer can jump straight to the frame that matters without waiting on a full download. For assets with multiple cuts or regional versions, version history keeps every iteration layered on the same asset record instead of scattered across files named final_v6, with a side-by-side compare view to check two versions or play two edits against each other before approving one.

Shared pages and a template gallery, not just a screening link

A Wiredrive quicklink is functional. A Playbook published page is a branded webpage: pick from 1000+ free templates, customize typography, cover image, and logo in a drag-and-drop page builder, and publish a client gallery, portfolio, or brand portal in one click. The internal board and the public page share the same content, so edits after a note session show up for the client without a re-send. A slideshow/presentation mode plays the board full-screen for a pitch, and a QR code makes it easy to hand off in person.

The Vault Stock, a stock media library with 3,000+ monthly users, embedded a published Playbook page directly into its Squarespace site and gated it behind membership with secure share links. "Playbook really ticks all the boxes for us. It's made our workflow smoother," said Anna Hamilton, per the Vault Stock story.

Build your own workflow with the Playbook API and MCP

Wiredrive has no public API. Playbook's REST API and MCP support turn the library into a backend you can build on: read and mutate assets, boards, comments, and search programmatically, or connect an MCP-capable agent (Claude, Cursor, and others) so it can organize uploads, run searches, and manage boards from a prompt. Point an agent at your production library to auto-file dailies as they land, trigger a review request when a cut is approved, or sync approved assets into a delivery system, without waiting on a vendor roadmap to ship the integration for you.

Pricing and access

Playbook publishes every tier: Free (100GB, 300 assets, no card), Pro at $120/year, Team at $240/year/member (video frame search, video frame scrubbing, and AI tagging start here), Business at $4,800/year flat with unlimited members and version history, and custom Enterprise. Guests comment, review, and download on every plan without taking a paid seat. No sales call required to see a number.

What to look for in a Wiredrive alternative

  1. Does pricing scale without a quote? A published price per tier means you can budget before you talk to sales.
  2. Can you search inside a video, not just for it? If footage older than a week is unfindable without scrubbing, review speed will not fix your bigger problem.
  3. Is feedback pinned to a frame or a pixel? Vague, file-level comments create round-trips that precise timecoded and positioned comments avoid.
  4. What happens after approval? A tool built only for review needs a hand-off to wherever the asset lives next. One library that holds review, search, and delivery removes that step.
  5. Can you build on it? An API and MCP layer means the tool adapts to your workflow instead of the other way around.
