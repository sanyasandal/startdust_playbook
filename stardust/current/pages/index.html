How does Playbook MCP work?

  1. You

    Post house just dropped the Scene 4 delivery into the workspace. Help me sort clips into Scenes selections, stills into Images, anything branded into Collateral.

  2. Playbook

    Reading 16 new assets against the project boards...

  3. Playbook

    Eight clips into Scene selections, three stills into Images, five into Collateral. The two re-renders of shots went in as versions. Nothing overwritten.

  1. You
    Attachment SS26_product_sheet.csv

    Attached the SS26 product sheet. Match column A to the filenames and fill in the fields.

  2. Playbook

    Reading 96 rows, matching against 1,284 assets...

  3. Playbook

    Matched 1,190 by SKU and wrote collection, material, and room onto each. 94 had no SKU in the filename, so those are on a board for you to assign.

  1. You

    The vendor went through the 'Retouch queue' board and left some comments. Can you summarize the changes and set status 'Approved' on the asset they actually signed off?

  2. Playbook

    Reading 14 comments left in Retouch queue…

  3. Playbook

    8 marked as 'Approved' and 2 marked for 'In progress' based on what they wrote. Five I couldn't call either way, so those are untouched and grouped for you.

  1. You

    Client wants to see where we're heading with the campaign. Pull the a few latest art direction, teaser, and event work into a board I can share with them.

  2. Playbook

    Reading 342 assets across the three campaign boards...

  3. Playbook

    38 on a new board, split the same three ways as the originals. 9 in Art directions, the teaser, and 28 in PR + Events. Ready to share when you are.

  1. You

    Half of this board is iterations that got uploaded as separate files instead of versions. Can you replace them?

  2. Playbook

    Reading 8 assets, matching by scene and pass...

  3. Playbook

    5 folded onto their originals as new versions. Every earlier pass is still there and any of them can roll back. Two stayed put since I couldn't match to anything.
