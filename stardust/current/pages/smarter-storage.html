A Visual Workspace
Beyond the Messy Folders.

Experience a living, breathing library designed for the way creative work actually happens.

See it in action

Real-time upload
with high-res previews

Drag, drop and forget about it. Playbook handles heavy lifting in the background, generating stunning, high-resolution previews instantly. Whether it's a 2GB RAW file or quick PNG, you finally see the beauty of your work the moment it touches your workspace.

In Playbook, you can also add HEX codes, live URLs, and notes right alongside your assets.

Grouping = Zero Clutter

Group similar formats, iterations, or files type into elegant "stacks." One thumbnail on the outside, your entire process stays intact on the inside. It's organization that keeps your gallery present-ready at any moment, especially when it's last-minute.

Playbook's visual intelligent automatically detects duplicates and automatically merge or group them.

Flexible View Modes

Your work isn't one-size-fits-all, so your view shouldn't be either. In Playbook, you can switch between stunning Gallery for browsing, List for displaying more details, or Kanban to keep track of your projects.

Tags & Custom fields

Easily add any custom #tags to your assets or use our built-in Custom fields to categorize assets by "Locations," "Vibe," "Client." Our deep-search metadata ensures that if you can think of it, you can find it in seconds.

Move at the speed of a click

Need to copy 500 assets to a new board, update their tags and change their permissions? Do it all at once. Our batch action tools are built for scale, allowing your to manage your entire libraries with the same effort it takes to manage a single file.
